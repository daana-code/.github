# Daana

**Model once, generate everything.**

Daana is a declarative data platform. Declare your sources, your business and your metrics in YAML. Daana generates the warehouse tables and transformations, with definitions your team and AI agents can trace from any metric back to its source.

## Explore Daana

- [See how the platform works](https://daana.dev/) or [watch the overview film](https://youtu.be/PLqFOr3Iwac).
- [Inspect the bookstore declarations](https://daana.dev/llms.txt): an overview and links to five example YAML files covering source contracts, a business model, a mapping and a revenue metric.
- [Read the documentation](https://docs.daana.dev/) for concepts, schemas and commands.
- [Explore the blog](https://blog.daana.dev/) for articles on declarative data platforms, modelling, lineage and agentic analytics.
